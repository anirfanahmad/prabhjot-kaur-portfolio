About

Driven by stories, built on curiosity.

I am Prabhjot Kaur, a student at CDLPU currently pursuing the LPU regularization pathway. My academic journey is shaped by discipline, adaptability, and the ambition to build something meaningful alongside my studies.

Beyond the classroom, I run Global Cover Story — a magazine-style platform where I feature articles, personal profiles, and inspiring journeys. I believe everyone has a story worth reading, and my work is to find those stories and present them beautifully.

Whether it is writing, editing, profiling people, or managing a content-driven brand, I enjoy turning ideas into polished, shareable work.
