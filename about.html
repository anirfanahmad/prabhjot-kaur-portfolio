About

Student, entrepreneur, storyteller.

Prabhjot Kaur is building a space where real stories get the editorial treatment they deserve.

From the classroom to the cover page.

Prabhjot Kaur is an entrepreneur and student at Lovely Professional University (LPU). While pursuing her degree, she founded Global Cover Story, an independent business and technology magazine that publishes original profiles, interviews, and long-form features.

Her work sits at the intersection of education, media, and entrepreneurship. She believes that every founder, professional, and changemaker has a story worth reading — and her magazine is the platform that brings those stories forward.

Global Cover Story covers business, technology, entrepreneurship, sustainability, healthcare, education, women in leadership, and more. The magazine releases new editions weekly and maintains a growing archive of featured leaders.

"Real stories deserve real context."

— Prabhjot Kaur, Founder
